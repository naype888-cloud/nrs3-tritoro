RS^N³·T³ · The baryon window on the ring

The window (1 − 1/c) e^{−1/c} needs c > 1: c = 1 gives 0 (the quantum saturates). On the open axis, at its Szegő limit C_N^∞ ≈ 1.1355, it is 0.0495, inside the measured Ω_b. On the ring, the limit is c = √2: the window is 0.1444, positive because the limit does not saturate. What the window needs is c > 1; Ω_b, w and c enter as measured data and the dark part is 1 − Ω_b.
